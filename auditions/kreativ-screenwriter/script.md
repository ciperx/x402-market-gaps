# The Bundle of Sticks

**Total running time: 64 seconds.** Ten shots. The longest shot is 7 seconds, so every scene can be generated as one AI video shot of 8 seconds or less.

**Premise:** Two quarrelling children learn from their father and a bundle of kindling that they are stronger held together.

**Look and sound:** Warm storybook realism. Honey, moss and dusk blue. Soft grain, dust in the air, no hard shadows. One camera move and one clear action per shot. The score is a wooden flute and a soft cello; they begin apart and end on the same three notes.

**Source:** Aesop, The Bundle of Sticks, Perry Index 53, public domain.

**Why this fable:** The Bundle of Sticks is in the public domain and far less performed than the Tortoise and the Hare, yet its lesson is physical. A child can see the cause and the result in the same pair of hands: one stick snaps, many sticks hold. There is no villain and no chase, only a small quarrel at the end of a working day. I moved the fable off the traditional deathbed and into a farmyard evening, because a dying father is not a picture for four to eight year olds. The last image repeats the first: two children, once pulling apart, now tied together like the two sticks in the cart.


## Characters

**PAPA**
- Look: Late thirties, broad shoulders, warm brown skin, grey at the temples, linen shirt with rolled sleeves, sawdust on his hands.
- Voice: Low and unhurried, with a smile behind the words. He never raises it.
- Business: He works while he talks, tying, lifting, wiping his hands on a cloth.

**ANA**
- Look: Nine years old, tall for her age, dark curls tied back, patched blue dress, scraped knuckles, one sock slipped down.
- Voice: Quick and certain, then quiet when she is wrong.
- Business: She plants her feet before she tries anything, as if the ground might move.

**SAM**
- Look: Six years old, small and round cheeked, oversized green jumper, one bootlace always loose.
- Voice: High and bright. He asks why more often than he answers.
- Business: He uses both hands for everything, even when one would do.
